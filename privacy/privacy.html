Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 • TouchDare for iOS

Summary

TouchDare does not collect, track, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal information, multi-touch input coordinates, or custom gameplay data. Everything runs locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how TouchDare ("we", "our", or "the App") operates regarding user privacy and data security. We respect your privacy rights and are committed to ensuring complete transparency regarding data handling. By using TouchDare, you agree to the minimal operational parameters set forth in this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

TouchDare is strictly an on-device party game application. As such:

  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • Touch Input Data: Multi-touch screen coordinates used during finger selection rounds are processed strictly in real-time within your device's active memory (RAM). Touch points are immediately discarded when fingers lift off the display screen and are never logged, recorded, or transmitted.
  • Custom Game Data: Any custom dares, tier selections, or countdown timers created or modified in the app settings are stored locally on your physical iOS device using system storage APIs. They are never backed up to remote servers operated by us.

3. Analytics and Tracking

TouchDare does not integrate third-party advertising frameworks, tracking cookies, software development kits (SDKs), behavioral profiling tools, or analytics software (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). We do not track your activity across other mobile applications or websites.

4. Third-Party Services

The App operates as a self-contained local binary on iOS. No external network connections or API calls are initialized during game execution. No user data is transmitted or shared with external third-party entities, advertisers, or software vendors.

5. Children's Privacy

Because TouchDare does not collect or solicit any personal information from users of any age, the App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy protection guidelines.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to maintain compliance with legal requirements or iOS platform updates. Any updates will be published directly to this web address with a revised effective date.
